Frequently Asked Questions

Is AI Career Copilot free to use?

Yes, AI Career Copilot is free for students. Create an account and start your career development journey today.

How does the ATS resume scoring work?

Our system extracts text from your PDF or DOCX resume, analyzes keywords, skills, sections, formatting, and action verbs to calculate a comprehensive ATS compatibility score.

Are the interview questions the same every time?

No, our AI generates unique questions based on your target role, difficulty level, and previous performance. Each interview is a fresh experience.

Can I track my progress over time?

Yes, every assessment, interview, and resume analysis is stored. Your career progress page shows real graphs of your scores over time, including improvements and declines.
