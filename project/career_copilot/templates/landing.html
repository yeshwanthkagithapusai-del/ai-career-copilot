[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ]

Answers to common inquiries.

[ 01 ] Is AI Career Copilot free to use for students?
Yes. AI Career Copilot is free for learners, students, and early-career developers. Simply create your account to run resume analyses, take assessments, and generate your learning roadmap.
[ 02 ] How does the ATS scoring engine evaluate my resume?
Our parser extracts full text from PDF and DOCX documents and benchmarks your content against canonical industry skill ontologies, measuring keyword density, section organization, action verbs, and quantifiable impact metrics.
[ 03 ] Are mock interview questions unique for each session?
Yes. Every mock interview generates custom questions tailored to your chosen target role, seniority level, and previous performance weaknesses so you never face repetitious drills.
[ 04 ] How does the skill evidence architecture work?
Rather than relying on self-reported skills, the platform collects verifiable evidence from multiple sources: resume analysis, assessment test scores, and interview performance, unifying them into canonical skill proficiencies.
